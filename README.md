## <img src="assets/emotes/hackergers.gif" alt="Hackergers" height="28"> Featured projects

### [Mulsanne](https://github.com/szpolny/mulsanne) · [mulsanne.xyz](https://mulsanne.xyz/)
Customizable in-game overlay and telemetry companion for **Le Mans Ultimate**. HUD widgets and live telemetry for sim racers.
<br>`Rust` `Tauri` `Svelte`

### [Outlap](https://github.com/szpolny/outlap) · [outlap.lol](https://outlap.lol)
Motorsport schedules for **F1, MotoGP, WEC, IndyCar, WRC** and more, shown in your local time, with Apple Calendar feeds that keep themselves up to date.
<br>`TypeScript` `SvelteKit` 

### [linuxz](https://github.com/szpolny/linuxz)
**DayZ launcher for Linux.** Launch the game with mods through Steam + Proton.
<br>`Rust` `Linux` `Proton`
